Printing your maps

Each ZIP contains five JPGs. The file ending in _full is the map at its native shape, up to 7200 pixels on the long edge, which prints clean at 24 inches. The _2x3, _3x4 and _4x5 files are pre-fitted for standard frames (12x18, 18x24, 24x36 use 2x3; 12x16 and 18x24 mats use 3x4; 8x10 and 16x20 use 4x5). The _iso file fits A4, A3, A2 and A1 frames. Upload any of them to your local print shop or an online printer and order at 300 DPI. Matte paper suits these maps best.
