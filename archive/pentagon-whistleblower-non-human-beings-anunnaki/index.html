Non-Human Beings Are Here. The Anunnaki Tablets Said It First

By Jordan Vale · November 25, 2026 · 7 min read

A Pentagon intelligence officer swore under oath that non-human beings are here, and the Anunnaki tablets said it first. On July 26, 2023, former Air Force intelligence officer David Grusch told the United States Congress that the government holds recovered craft built by non-human intelligence, along with what he called non-human biologics. Five thousand years earlier, the Sumerians of Nippur and Uruk recorded gods who lived among them, ate at their tables, advised their kings, and then moved below the earth behind sealed gates.

Read the two records side by side and you stop seeing a myth and a hearing. You see one story told twice. The scribes wrote it in clay. Grusch told it into a microphone. Both describe beings who never left, a small circle allowed inside, and a closed door for everyone else.

What David Grusch Swore Under Oath

Grusch served fourteen years as an Air Force officer and worked at the National Reconnaissance Office and the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency, both inside the Defense Department. He represented his agency on the Pentagon's UAP Task Force. That is the man who raised his right hand before the House Oversight Subcommittee on National Security, the Border, and Foreign Affairs.

He told the committee the United States runs a decades-long program to recover and reverse engineer crashed craft. He said the program holds intact and partially intact vehicles. Asked about the pilots, he said biologics came with some of the recoveries, and that the people who handled them called them non-human.

His complaint had already cleared the Intelligence Community Inspector General, who in 2022 found it credible and urgent. The same month he testified, Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer and Senator Mike Rounds filed the UAP Disclosure Act, which wrote the phrase "non-human intelligence" into federal legislation and called for the government to take control of recovered technologies of unknown origin. Three receipts, three institutions, one month.

The Mountain House at Nippur Had a Dinner Table

Go back to Nippur in southern Iraq, the holy city of Sumer. Its central temple was the Ekur, a name that translates as Mountain House. It was the home of Enlil, lord of the Anunnaki, and kings from Naram-Sin of Akkad to Ur-Nammu of Ur poured fortunes into rebuilding it.

The temple ran on a menu. Ur III administrative tablets from the livestock center at Puzrish-Dagan log the sheep, oxen and beer sent to Nippur as regular offerings for the gods. At Uruk, a ritual tablet now in the Louvre (AO 6451) lays out four daily meals for the god Anu and his consort Antu, morning and evening, with set portions of bread, meat and wine.

You keep a kitchen running for someone who eats. Priesthoods across three thousand years of Mesopotamian history staffed, supplied and scheduled meals for residents of the house. A people who invented writing, accounting and the calendar did not keep ledgers for an empty room.

You do not set a table for a myth. You set a table for a guest who is still in the house.

The Uruk Tablet That Pairs Every King With a Sage

In the 1959 to 1960 season, German excavators working the Bit Resh temple at Uruk pulled out a clay tablet catalogued as W 20030,7. Scholars call it the Uruk List of Kings and Sages. Its scribe, Anu-belshunu, dated it to 165 BC.

The tablet pairs each ruler with an advisor who stood beside him. The first king, Ayalu, is paired with Uanna, the fish-cloaked sage the Greeks knew as Oannes, the being who rose from the sea and taught humanity writing, law and building. Seven antediluvian kings get seven apkallu sages. After the Flood the pairing continues, king by king, down to Esarhaddon of Assyria in the seventh century BC.

That is an unbroken line of non-human counsel running through the whole of recorded kingship. The tablet records a chain of advisors standing behind the throne and never records a departure. The Epic of Erra tells you where they went. Marduk says he sent the sages down to the Apsu, the deep waters beneath the earth, and did not order their return. They were relocated below.

Seven Judges Below the Earth

The Descent of Inanna survives on tablets excavated at Nippur, many now held at the Penn Museum in Philadelphia. In it, the goddess Inanna passes through seven gates into the underworld. At every gate the gatekeeper Neti strips her of one item of power until she stands bare.

Waiting at the bottom are the Anunna, the seven judges, seated before Ereshkigal. They fix their eyes on Inanna and pass sentence. These are the same Anunnaki who ran the Mountain House, now holding court underground.

Look at the architecture of that text. Seven checkpoints. Everything you carry is taken at the door. A seated panel decides who walks back out. The Sumerians described a secured facility under the earth and told you exactly what clearance it took to get in.

The Same Floor Plan, Five Thousand Years Apart

Grusch told Congress he could name programs, locations and people, but only inside a SCIF, a sensitive compartmented information facility. A sealed room. A short list of cleared names. In March 2024, the Pentagon's AARO office published a report saying it found no verified recovered craft in the records it reviewed. That report proves Grusch's point. His testimony was that the program sits outside the records oversight bodies are allowed to see. An office that only searches the open files will always come back empty from a compartment built to stay off them.

The Sumerians never wrote that the Anunnaki went home. They wrote that the gods moved below, behind seven gates, attended by a priesthood that fed them and a line of kings who took their advice. Grusch walked into a congressional hearing room in 2023 and described a hidden program, a cleared few, and beings who never left.

Frequently Asked

What did David Grusch tell Congress about non-human beings?

On July 26, 2023, under oath before the House Oversight Committee, Grusch said the United States holds recovered craft of non-human origin and that some recoveries included non-human biologics. The Intelligence Community Inspector General had already judged his complaint credible and urgent.

Who were the Anunnaki?

The Anunnaki were the ruling gods of Sumer, led by Enlil from the Ekur, the Mountain House at Nippur. Sumerian texts place them in temples among humans and, in the Descent of Inanna, seated as seven judges below the earth.

Do Sumerian tablets say the gods left Earth?

The tablets record the opposite. The Uruk List of Kings and Sages pairs rulers with divine advisors all the way to the seventh century BC, and the Epic of Erra says the sages were sent down into the Apsu, beneath the earth, and never ordered back.

How does Grusch's testimony match the Anunnaki texts?

Both describe non-human beings kept in hidden places, contact limited to a small cleared circle, and a closed door for the public. The seven gates of the underworld and the sealed SCIF Grusch asked to testify in serve the same purpose.
