The CIA Sealed This Book for 50 Years: The Pole Shift Inside

By Jordan Vale · September 16, 2026 · 7 min read

The CIA classified a geology book. That sentence is not a theory. It is a documented fact sitting in the agency's own reading room, stamped with a declassification date of June 25, 2013.

The book is called The Adam and Eve Story. Chan Thomas wrote it in 1963. The agency held it for fifty years. They released part of it. The rest is still blacked out. The question that combination raises has never been answered.

Chan Thomas Wrote It in 1963

Thomas held a background in physics and engineering. In 1963 he published a manuscript titled The Adam and Eve Story: The History of Cataclysms. The book did not traffic in prophecy or scripture. It described a physical mechanism laid out in chapters, with equations and geological references. Thomas argued that the earth's outer crust periodically slips over the underlying mantle. The driver is ice mass accumulation at the poles, building rotational imbalance until the crust can no longer hold its position against the momentum. When the slip occurs, Thomas calculated it in days. The sequence of consequences he described follows the physics of a planet whose surface has just moved out from under its oceans. He cited published geological literature at each stage. He submitted the manuscript for publication in 1963. Sometime after that, the Central Intelligence Agency acquired a copy and placed it in restricted archives. It sat there until 2013.

The Six-Thousand-Foot Wave

Thomas described a hydraulic surge. When the crust repositions abruptly, the oceans cannot track the movement instantaneously. The result is a wall of displaced water. Thomas calculated the figure at six thousand feet, advancing at roughly a thousand miles an hour across entire continents. He did not derive this from mythology. He pointed at three categories of physical evidence already catalogued and sitting in research institutions. Marine sediment deposits found hundreds of miles from any current coastline. Whale bones recovered from inland terrain at elevations inconsistent with any gradual sea-level mechanism. Thick layers of ocean-floor material laid down on top of continental landmasses. The University of Michigan Museum of Natural History holds cetacean specimens recovered from landlocked Michigan at elevations that standard Holocene sea-level models cannot account for. Thomas was not asking anyone to take his word for the wave. He was asking why the evidence for it was sitting unconnected in museum storage.

The CIA does not classify geology. Except once. And that exception describes a six-thousand-foot wave crossing North America in a single afternoon.

Why the CIA Classified a Geology Book

The Central Intelligence Agency collects intelligence and analyzes threats to national security. It does not peer-review geology manuscripts or regulate scientific publishing. It classified The Adam and Eve Story sometime after 1963 and held it as a restricted document for fifty years. The CIA's own declassification record, accessible through the agency's Electronic Reading Room, confirms this. The agency identified the document during a mandatory review process and released a version of it in 2013. That act of release is simultaneously the confirmation that they held it. There is no external accusation required. The CIA's own administrative record establishes that a government intelligence agency treated a manuscript about catastrophic pole shifts as material warranting classification and restriction. The question that follows is not whether Thomas was credible. The question is what calculation a national security agency made when it decided that a geology book needed to be kept away from the public for five decades.

What They Released and What They Kept

The 2013 release was partial. The version accessible in the CIA Electronic Reading Room runs to 57 pages. The original manuscript Thomas published in 1963 ran to 284 pages. The gap between those two numbers is material the agency reviewed under Executive Order 13526 and determined qualified for continued restriction on national security grounds. That executive order requires the justification to be specific and documented. The CIA met the bar for the redacted sections. Scanned copies of Thomas's 1963 original circulate online, and a comparison against the CIA's released version identifies what was cut. The redacted passages fall in the chapters where Thomas works through the mechanism's trigger conditions and the timeline for recurrence. The agency released the historical argument about past events. They kept the chapters that deal with the clock.

The Geological Record Confirms His Core Timeline

The Younger Dryas Boundary layer is a documented stratigraphic feature confirmed by independent laboratories on four continents. At approximately 10,900 BC, something happened across North America that left a distinctive black mat at that precise depth in the soil record. Analysis of that layer has identified nano-diamonds, magnetic spherules, iridium enrichment, and high-temperature melt glass. These are signatures of a high-energy surface event. The Clovis culture, the most archaeologically widespread human population identified in pre-Columbian North America, disappears from the record at that exact boundary. Their tool sites end. Their kill sites end. Their bones end. The Smithsonian's National Museum of Natural History holds Clovis-era collection materials that terminate at that layer. Thomas did not fabricate the anomaly. He built a mechanism around physical evidence that geologists have spent sixty years assigning to competing causes without producing a settled explanation. The boundary is real. The disappearance is real. The debate is still open.

Thomas Put a Date on the Last Cataclysm

Thomas did not write 'sometime in the distant past.' He calculated a date. He placed the most recent catastrophic pole repositioning at approximately 9,500 BC. That figure falls within the uncertainty range of the Younger Dryas Boundary at 10,900 BC. It also aligns with a source that predates Thomas by 2,300 years. Plato wrote the Timaeus in 360 BC. In it, an Egyptian priest tells the Athenian statesman Solon that a great civilization was destroyed by catastrophic flooding 9,000 years before Solon's visit, placing the event at approximately 9,600 BC. Three data points. A Greek philosophical dialogue written in 360 BC. A physical boundary layer confirmed by laboratory analysis across four continents. An American engineer's classified manuscript written in 1963. They converge on the same century. The CIA classified the one that named the mechanism and calculated the interval between events.

The redacted sections have not been released. Whatever Thomas wrote in those chapters, the agency's 2013 declassification review determined that they met the threshold for continued restriction under national security justification. Executive Order 13526 requires that determination to be specific, documented, and defensible. The CIA made it. For a geology book. About ancient cataclysms. In 2013. That justification was accepted and the pages stayed black.

The magnetic north pole has moved more than 1,400 miles since 1900. The rate of that movement has accelerated in the last two decades at a pace with no recorded parallel in the modern observation period. If Thomas's mechanism is real, and if the chapters the CIA redacted contain his calculation of when the next slip is due, the question is no longer whether you believe Chan Thomas. The question is what the people who classified his work believe. And whether the answer is in those pages they decided you were not ready to read.
